A NEW WORLD BEGINS

Bringing you GENESIS.

From the developer who played a key part in making Kathana's new engine possible comes a completely new experience. Every system and line of code is being written from the ground up on zEngine. Only classic game assets—such as models and textures—are reused to preserve the world's recognisable identity.

I

Built from the ground up

GENESIS is a complete rewrite developed on zEngine as a new foundation for the entire experience.

II

Classic at its heart

The familiar gameplay, atmosphere and social adventure remain at the centre, supported by modern functions.

III

A stronger online world

New network handling and server improvements are designed to support a more stable, responsive journey.

IV

Expanded everywhere

New maps will broaden the world, with Windows, Linux, macOS, iPhone and Android included in the platform vision.
