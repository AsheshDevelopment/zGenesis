Foundation

Delivery principles

01

Source-faithful experience

Characters, controls, world behaviour, interfaces, combat and progression are reconstructed from the available game evidence.

02

Authoritative rules

Important actions are validated consistently so every player observes the same result.

03

Safe progress

Character, item, currency and quest progress survives reconnects, maintenance and recoverable failures.

04

Careful content

Original game assets are not published here. Testers must use an authorised local installation.

05

Evidence first

A visible feature is not complete until its full interaction and recovery paths have been tested.

06

Bounded beta

A complete smaller experience is validated before the historical catalogue and new GENESIS content expand.

Post-beta

Scope held beyond the first closed beta

The first closed beta deliberately excludes unrestricted public registration, production-scale discovery, the complete historical map and quest catalogue, large-scale Ashram warfare, premium billing, private shops, and the full mount and pet catalogue. New GENESIS regions, mobile releases and optional systems will expand only after the bounded beta route is stable.

Policy

Public roadmap updates

  • Completed tasks require recorded test evidence.
  • Failed requirements remain visible until corrected.
  • New source discoveries may change scope before implementation begins.
  • Playable client and server stability remain the immediate development priority.
  • Progress percentages change only when implementation and verification evidence support the change.
  • Closed-beta accounts now include verification, recovery, optional two-factor protection, controlled admission, agreement acceptance, service status and cached rankings.
  • Role-based administrator sign-in now requires two-factor authentication, records append-only audit history and supports audited content, account-sanction, beta-admission, support and guarded operations workflows; live game-session revocation and full browser-flow coverage remain in development.
  • Public Ashram and community pages are available, and the first account-linked support and known-issues workflow passes its authenticated database and HTTP-level checks.
  • Updates will not expose private infrastructure, account details, private development details or original asset contents.
  • Closed-beta dates will be announced only after the entry requirements are met.
